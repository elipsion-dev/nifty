How the conversion works

Every unit is converted through meters as a common base: your value is multiplied by that unit's length in meters, then divided by the target unit's length in meters. That is the standard, exact way to convert between length units — 1 inch is defined as exactly 2.54 centimeters, 1 foot is 12 inches, 1 yard is 3 feet, and 1 mile is 5,280 feet — so the results here match any other correctly-built converter.

Common conversions this handles

Feet to centimeters or meters for furniture, shipping, or international spec sheets; inches to millimeters for parts and hardware; meters to feet for room or building dimensions; miles to kilometers for travel distances and race lengths; and yards to meters for fabric, lawns, or athletic fields. For a length-times-width area instead of a single conversion, use the Square Footage Calculator, which lets the length and width each use a different unit.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert feet to centimeters?

Set "From unit" to feet and "To unit" to centimeters, then enter your value. 1 foot equals exactly 30.48 centimeters, and the headline result updates as you type.

How many centimeters are in a meter?

100. Set the value to 1, "From unit" to meters, and read the centimeters row in the reference table — or set "From unit" to centimeters and "To unit" to meters for the reverse direction.

How do I convert miles to kilometers?

Set "From unit" to miles and "To unit" to kilometers. 1 mile equals about 1.60934 kilometers, so 5 miles converts to roughly 8.05 kilometers.

Can I see a value in every unit at once instead of picking one target unit?

Yes — the table below the headline result always lists the entered value converted into all eight supported units, regardless of which "To unit" is selected.
